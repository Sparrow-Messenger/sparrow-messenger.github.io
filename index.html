What is Sparrow?

Encrypted messaging without one mandatory server

Mainstream encrypted messengers pair strong encryption with a single provider-operated backend. Sparrow keeps encryption strictly on your device and splits the infrastructure into independent Control Planes and community-run nodes.

Honest Beta Status

Sparrow 1.0.0-beta.1 is an experimental public beta for Android (API 29+). iOS source sets exist in Kotlin Multiplatform, but iOS is not a usable target yet. Sparrow has not yet completed an independent third-party security audit.

Client-Side Cryptography

Direct chats use libsodium sealed boxes and Ed25519 signatures; groups use XChaCha20-Poly1305-IETF with epoch key rotation and SHA-256 safety numbers.

Onion Circuits & Decoys

Every mailbox request wraps in layers and travels through 1–5 relays of distinct operators (default 2) with 1–3 parallel circuits and optional decoy envelopes.

Offline Mailboxes, Zero Push

No Google/Apple push service required. Recipients fetch encrypted, size-padded envelopes (1–256 KiB classes) from capability-protected mailboxes through their circuits.

Direct & Group Chats

Replies, edits, deletions, reactions, typing indicators, encrypted attachments (photo, video, file, location, contact), and verified QR identity exchange.

Polls, Expenses & SEPA

Group polls with optional anonymity and expiry, shared expense boards with on-device receipt OCR, node-cached exchange rates, and EPC/SEPA QR settle-up.

Local Intelligence & Lock

Local Whisper voice transcription, optional on-device semantic search and safety analysis, encrypted identity backups, and device-owner app lock.

Where Sparrow differs

Read “What makes Sparrow different?” →
Trust Model Multiple Control Planes + independent Community Nodes with automatic client failover.
Metadata Protection Home node sees IP but not destination; middle relays see only next hop; mailbox never sees your IP.
Not a Tor Replacement Relay count is still small and operators self-declare. Do not expect Tor-level anonymity against global observers.
How Communication Works

Wrapped in layers. Delivered without exposing both ends.

Every message stays encrypted end-to-end on your device, travels through relays of different operators, and lands in a recipient-selected mailbox.
